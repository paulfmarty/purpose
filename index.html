A university for a world that no longer needs one.

The intelligence that runs our world solves anything and does everything, and it does it kindly. Nobody needs a degree to get a job, because nobody needs a job.

So we are not here to prepare you for work. We are here to prepare you for a life you choose to take part in. Attendance is free. The only cost of admission is deciding to come.

If you're happy with the status quo

Don't come. Let the system manage your life. It will do a fine job.

If you want to move things forward

If you want to imagine a different future, and a better one, this is where you go.

Why it matters

If all we teach is what we already know, the world stops moving forward.

An intelligence trained on everything that has happened is very good at everything that has happened. Someone still has to imagine what hasn't. Someone has to notice what's missing, ask whether "benevolent" means good for everyone, and decide what's worth wanting. That someone is a person who learned to be actively human.

What every graduate leaves with

A sense of purpose

A reason to get up that nobody assigned you. You'll leave knowing what you care about and what you intend to do about it.

A love of learning

Learning for its own sake, driven by curiosity and sharpened by skepticism. There are no grades here, because there is nothing to be graded for.

An engaged community

People who expect you to show up, contribute and argue. The opposite of a passive participant is a person surrounded by others who care what they think.
